easyjob Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Overview

easyjob helps users search for jobs, prepare local resume drafts, and open verified employer application pages. The app is designed to be local-first and does not require an account.

Data stored on your device

Profile details, saved jobs, generated resume versions, application notes, follow-up dates, feedback signals, settings, and local usage counters are stored on your device using iOS local storage.

Data collected by easyjob

easyjob does not collect personal data on a developer-operated server, does not sell user data, and does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Job feed and employer pages

The app downloads a public job feed from GitHub Pages and may open verified employer application pages inside the app. If you choose to submit information to an employer, that employer may collect and process the information under its own privacy policy.

Resume and message generation

Resume and message generation uses templates and local matching rules. Your resume content is not sent to an AI API by this app.

User control

You can reset local profile, resume, saved job, tracker, feedback, and metric data from the Profile tab in the app. Deleting the app also removes app-local data from the device.

Contact

For help, privacy questions, or deletion requests, open the easyjob Support page.
